How to connect
  1. Turn on the meter and pair LuxMeter in your device's Bluetooth settings.
  2. Press Connect to meter and choose LuxMeter. On Windows, choose the Outgoing COM port (Bluetooth settings, More Bluetooth options, COM Ports).
  3. A reading arrives about every half second. The log stays in this browser until you clear it, so download the CSV to keep it.

Works in Chrome or Edge on Windows, and Chrome 138 or newer on Android (open the page from an https:// link).
